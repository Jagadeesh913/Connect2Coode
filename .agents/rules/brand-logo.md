# Official Brand Logo Policy

Always use the official **Connect to Code** logo assets provided by the user. Do not replace, remove, or substitute them with generic placeholders, standard SVGs, or external images.

## Official Assets
- Full Official Logo: `/logo.png` and `/logo-full.png` (Transparent PNG with 3D gradient ribbon `< >` / infinity loop mark, "CONNECTTOCODE" heading, and "CODE TOGETHER CONNECT FOREVER" tagline)
- Horizontal Lockup: `/logo-horizontal.png` (Ribbon mark on the left, typography on the right)
- Brand Mark Only: `/logo-mark.png` and `/logo-mark-transparent.png` (Isolated 3D gradient ribbon `< >` mark)
- Favicons: `/favicon.ico`, `/favicon.svg`, `/favicon-32x32.png`, `/favicon-16x16.png`, `/apple-touch-icon.png` (Generated directly from the official brand mark)
- Reusable Component: `<BrandLogo />` located in `src/shared/components/ui/BrandLogo.tsx`.
